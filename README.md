# Shadow Arena — Space Combat V5

A browser-based endless 2D space survival game.

## V5
- 4 distinct astronaut characters: Cosmic Paladin, Void Stalker, Volt, Solar Nova
- 8 weapons: Gravity Saber, Star-Launcher, Nebula Cannon, Quantum Rifle, Dual Ion Daggers, Black Hole Scythe, Asteroid Rings, Gamma Staff
- 8 unique specials, two per character
- Special selection is separate from weapon selection and Space activates the chosen special
- Distinct special mechanics: radial slam, shield/invulnerability, directional barrage, black-hole pull, chain lightning, target lock strike, meteor shower, expanding solar flare
- Space mobs, scout ships, drones, asteroids and mothership boss remain supported
- Different procedural weapon sounds and special sounds
- Controls: WASD/Arrows move, mouse aim, left click attack, Shift dash, Space special

Replace `index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js` and `README.md` in the GitHub Pages repository.
